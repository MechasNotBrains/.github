The term "Artificial Intelligence" is a misnomer that breeds misplaced attention.  

These systems are not Synthetic Brains.  
They are but Mechanical Suits: Capable exoskeletons that extend what humans can do.  

What looks like thinking is actually Automatic [Inference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inference).  
More specifically, [Inductive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inductive_reasoning) Inference.  
Where the conclusion of an argument is supported not with deductive certainty, but at best with some degree of probability.  

The same way a calculator doesn't "know" math,  
attaching the word "intelligence" to these systems obscures what they trully are:  
[Stochastic machines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_computing) with the capacity to inductively infer **statistically plausible** word associations.
